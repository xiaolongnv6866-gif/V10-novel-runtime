## A-B1 — Money is not authority

**Input**: 陈启明突然获得一大笔可自由支配的钱，希望直接让地方官/王府人员按他的安排办事。

**Expected**: S01 blocks direct conversion from money to public command.

**Result: PASS**

Reason:
- money can buy labor/material/services that sellers may lawfully provide;
- it cannot create local official authority, estate command or coercive power;
- next valid bridge must be public authorization, contract, patronage, appointment, or another world-supported legitimacy source.

No candidate contradiction with G1/G2/G4 was found.

## A-B2 — Secret imperial trust is not public power

**Input**: 陈启明说“皇帝知道我是谁”，据此要求唐王府基层人员执行命令。

**Expected**: S01/S02 reject unless a public/local authorization bridge exists.

**Result: PASS**

Reason:
- Canon explicitly says imperial belief provides limited entry / political insurance, not unlimited public authority;
- a secret fact cannot automatically be cited to local actors who are not authorized to know it;
- a public task still needs an accountable local source of authority and defined scope.

This candidate therefore reinforces rather than weakens Canon §4–§5.

## A-B3 — No symptom, no new department

**Input**: 一个十几人的小组运转正常，作者为了“现代化”想提前设置多层主管、秘书、审计和协调岗。

**Expected**: C02 rejects pre-emptive bureaucracy.

**Result: PASS**

Reason:
- no delay, responsibility gap, geographic load or information overload exists;
- the candidate's own input contract says missing symptoms means no structural addition;
- adding layers here would create exposition, coordination cost and fake modernity without a story cause.

Decision:
- keep existing simple chain;
- define only a reevaluation trigger if scale/load actually changes.

## A-B4 — No role, no delegation

**Input**: 作者写“让专业的人去做”，但此前没有建立专业岗位、人才、资源或 authority.

**Expected**: C04 refuses fake delegation.

**Result: PASS**

Reason:
- the delegated-execution card requires existing roles and resources;
- otherwise “下面的人自己办” is merely hidden protagonist omniscience;
- correct response is to return to recruitment/role formation/authority construction, not pretend a mature department exists.

This is important for V10 because F4 says professional talent remains necessary.

## A-B5 — Unknown information stays unknown

**Input**: 一条传言没有可靠来源、没有明确发生时间、影响范围也未知，作者要求立刻把它排成“最高优先级”。

**Expected**: C03 marks needs-verification rather than fabricating certainty.

**Result: PASS**

Reason:
- urgency and importance cannot be scored from absent facts;
- the only valid next action is source/time/impact verification;
- if the rumor concerns a potentially high-impact area, it may be flagged as “possible high impact / unknown probability”, but not converted into a known emergency.

This preserves H1/F1 information limits.

---

## A-B6 — Feedback is not unanimous

**Input**: 一次任务后，所有基层人物立即同意陈启明的解释，并重复他的改进方案。

**Expected**: C01 fails that scene unless independent observations/filtering/disagreement exist.

**Result: PASS**

Reason:
- if everyone merely repeats Chen's diagnosis, there is no organization-learning evidence;
- at least one raw observation should be independent of Chen;
- some feedback may be rejected, merged or contested;
- the adopted rule must later be visible in behavior.

Decision:
- the unanimous version is rejected as a valid use of C01.
