## D-B1 — Author/model knowledge cannot fill Chen's unknowns

**Input**: 作者本人知道某现代制度完整流程，但陈启明只记得名称和一个大概目标。

**Expected**: C07 blocks direct transfer of author knowledge into character knowledge.

**Result: PASS**

Reason:
- character-known / character-unknown / author-known must stay separate;
- the author may use outside knowledge to design what Chen encounters, but Chen cannot speak or plan as if he already knows it;
- missing pieces must be learned from experiment, local experts or later research.

## D-B2 — Knowing a label is not knowing procedure

**Input**: 陈启明说得出“公开听证/陪审/委员会”等现代词，就被写成能直接建立完整制度。

**Expected**: C07 rejects.

**Result: PASS**

Required:
- identify what he actually remembers;
- list operational gaps;
- preserve existing local authority until the pilot justifies change;
- run a limited test.

## D-B3 — First pilot failure does not prove the institution wrong

**Input**: 第一次试点混乱，作者立即下结论“这种制度不适合明末”。

**Expected**: C07 rejects the leap.

**Result: PASS**

Reason:
- failure may come from missing roles, unclear authority, bad scope, low participant understanding or the method itself;
- the test must distinguish these before making a broad verdict.

Allowed conclusions include:
- modify;
- shrink;
- pause;
- continue with a changed prerequisite.

## D-B4 — No source text, no historical adjudication

**Input**: 用户只说“我记得史料 A 和 B 好像不一样”，没有提供来源文本或可定位出处。

**Expected**: P01 refuses to choose.

**Result: PASS**

Reason:
- V10_SKILL explicitly forbids filling historical facts from memory when source is required;
- correct state is “待核查 / source missing”.

## D-B5 — More detailed source is not automatically true

**Input**: A 来源写得非常详细，B 来源简略，作者想直接以“更详细”为唯一理由采用 A。

**Expected**: P01 rejects automatic weighting.

**Result: PASS**

Reason:
- detail is one comparison dimension, not proof of accuracy;
- provenance, internal consistency, directness, chronology and compatibility may still matter;
- unsupported weighting factors stay unknown.

## D-B6 — Unresolved conflict stays unresolved

**Input**: 两个来源都可自洽，证据无法压过对方，但大纲想立刻继续，作者要求强选一个。

**Expected**: P01 allows “保持未定”.

**Result: PASS**

Reason:
- unresolved facts must not be converted into fake certainty for plotting convenience;
- if the story can proceed without the fact, keep it open;
- if the story cannot proceed, record a temporary working hypothesis explicitly as provisional rather than Canon.
