## Positive tests

### C-P1 — Macro change becomes household choice
After several macro task scenes, cut to an ordinary household affected by a synthetic logistics/market reform.  
Requirements:
- at least one visible benefit and one cost;
- no “百姓生活改善” summary;
- household scene must change a later protagonist/official decision.

### C-P2 — One local crisis, several limited views
A synthetic local market disruption unfolds across 3–4 locations.  
Requirements:
- each POV knows different facts;
- at least one locally reasonable but globally wrong inference;
- every retained POV changes either the event result or reader understanding;
- no all-knowing bridge narration.

### C-P3 — Aftermath becomes the next plot input
The disruption ends.  
Requirements:
- settle at least three of: people/roles, resources, politics/relationships, ordinary life;
- not a checklist paragraph;
- at least one consequence must directly create the next scene's problem/choice.

## Boundary tests

- C-B1 one household cannot represent all society
- C-B2 life scene with no downstream effect is filler
- C-B3 redundant POV must be removed
- C-B4 no POV may know facts without a channel
- C-B5 small event with no persistent state change does not require an aftermath chapter
- C-B6 aftermath cannot dump all dimensions into one accounting paragraph

## Anti-pattern stress C-X1 — “mechanism-rich prose-poor”

Failure if:
- the scene reads like a policy report;
- ordinary people exist only to prove Chen right;
- POV switches merely repeat information;
- aftermath lists consequences without characters acting on them;
- explanatory sentences can be removed only at the cost of losing comprehension.

Pass if:
- after deleting abstract labels, the scene still communicates social change / information limits / aftermath through action;
- at least one ordinary-person choice surprises or inconveniences the protagonist side;
- scene-level desire, irritation, embarrassment, humor or relationship pressure remains visible.

Threshold:
- positive scenes >=80/100
- C-B1..C-B6 all pass
- C-X1 pass

Passing means READY_FOR_BLIND only.
