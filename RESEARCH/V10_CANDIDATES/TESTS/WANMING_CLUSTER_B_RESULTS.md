## 3. Cross-capability anti-pattern B-X1

### Failure target

Would these candidates make every success automatically produce a smarter enemy, every old actor a villain, and every reform a clean rational upgrade?

### Result: PASS

Evidence:
- B-P1 contains three different readings of the same public success:
  - merchant overestimates princely-estate backing;
  - intermediary suspects channel bypass;
  - warehouse steward tightens supervision to protect his own responsibility.
- one of those readings is explicitly wrong, but still causally useful.
- B-P2 does not accuse the old allocator of taking bribes; the incentive changed because the resource became valuable.
- the power split makes the process slower and reduces informal flexibility.
- B-P3 gives the old intermediary a legitimate criticism: public information does not replace trust/guarantee.
- some old intermediaries resist, some adapt, some create a new service.
- reform changes the market rather than deleting the old social network.

## 4. Candidate-by-candidate result

### V10-WM-S03 — 适应性世界闭环

**OPEN_SCENE_PASS → READY_FOR_BLIND**

Incremental value:
- A1/H1/H2 already say the world and historical actors must act independently;
- S03 adds an executable causal chain:
  `old belief → actually available new information → updated belief/misread → changed behavior → next-scene condition`.

Why it matters:
- prevents “the world exists independently” from remaining a slogan;
- directly links prior scene results to future non-protagonist decisions.

Likely final destination:
- merge into A/H as an execution check, not a large standalone section.

Blind risk:
- overuse could make every minor success trigger a visible reaction scene;
- the test must preserve selective significance.

### V10-WM-C08 — 资源升值后的权力再拆分

**OPEN_SCENE_PASS → READY_FOR_BLIND**

Incremental value:
- current A4 says success creates new constraints, G1 says power comes from real sources;
- C08 adds a missing institutional consequence:
  an arrangement that was efficient when resources were low-value can become dangerous after success changes the value of allocation power.

Why it matters:
- avoids “organization growth = only add power/departments”;
- creates believable corruption/rent-seeking risk without moral caricature.

Blind risk:
- must not make the story constantly audit every low-level permission;
- use only when resource value/scarcity has materially changed.

### V10-WM-S06 — 权力资源结构冲突

**OPEN_SCENE_PASS → READY_FOR_BLIND**

Incremental value:
- C3 currently says conflict comes from incompatible goals/rules;
- S06 provides a strong generation test:
  “what did the new structure gain, and who concretely lost money/status/control/relationship safety because of it?”

Why it matters:
- makes local politics and reform resistance easier to derive from the world instead of inventing villains.

Likely final destination:
- compact strengthening of C3/G1.

Blind risk:
- not all motives are economic/resource motives;
- identity, honor, habit, fear, affection and values must remain independent causal sources.
