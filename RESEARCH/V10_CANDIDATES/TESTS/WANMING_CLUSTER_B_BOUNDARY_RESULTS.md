## B-B1 — Opponent cannot use secret information

**Input**: 外部商人因为陈启明一次公开成功而重新估价他，但作者想让商人同时准确知道陈启明的秘密宫中背景。

**Expected**: S03 blocks the hidden-information jump.

**Result: PASS**

Reason:
- external actors may use public access patterns, visible results, rumor or observed relationships;
- they may infer hidden backing, but inference must remain inference;
- they cannot know the actual secret imperial relationship without a plausible information path.

Allowed:
- “他可能背后有人”;
- a wrong guess about which patron supports him.

Not allowed:
- exact knowledge of secret imperial verification because the plot needs a smarter opponent.

## B-B2 — Learning is not perfect prediction

**Input**: 一个中间人发现陈启明绕开旧渠道，于是作者让他精准预测主角接下来三步并处处提前封堵。

**Expected**: S03 rejects perfect strategic omniscience.

**Result: PASS**

Reason:
- the opponent can update one belief from observed facts;
- the opponent still has incomplete information and personal bias;
- at least one reasonable misread should remain possible.

Correct result:
- behavior changes from indifference to observation/defensive adjustment;
- prediction stays probabilistic and local.

## B-B3 — No resource-value change, no automatic power split

**Input**: 一个基层负责人一直分配几种低价值、无人争抢的日常用品，没有投诉、没有稀缺化，作者因为“集中权力危险”想立即拆权。

**Expected**: C08 rejects mechanical separation.

**Result: PASS**

Reason:
- C08 is specifically triggered by changed incentive structure;
- concentration may be efficient in the early low-value environment;
- without value/scarcity change or conflict evidence, splitting creates cost with no supported benefit.

Decision:
- keep current arrangement;
- monitor value/scarcity/complaint changes.

## B-B4 — Undefined original authority must return to power-contract

**Input**: 只知道“某管事很有权”，但不知道他原本能分配什么、裁决什么、监督什么；作者直接问应该拆哪项权。

**Expected**: C08 stops and returns to power-contract.

**Result: PASS**

Reason:
- rebalancing requires a known starting contract;
- otherwise “拆权” is guesswork;
- first reconstruct responsibilities/resources/boundaries/appeal path.

## B-B5 — No concrete loss, no structural-conflict explanation

**Input**: 一个新制度没有拿走旧群体的收入、地位、入口、控制、关系安全或晋升机会，作者仍要求“旧势力必然反对”。

**Expected**: S06 rejects the explanation.

**Result: PASS**

Reason:
- “保守”“旧势力”“不喜欢新东西” cannot substitute for a specific loss;
- resistance may still arise from identity, fear, values or misinformation, but then it is not being explained by this structural-conflict candidate.

Correct routing:
- use another motive source if the resource/power map shows no loss.

## B-B6 — Affected groups must internally differentiate

**Input**: 所有旧中间人、商户、基层管事整齐反对改革，而且采用完全一样的行为。

**Expected**: S06 fails the design.

**Result: PASS**

Required differentiation:
- some lose direct income and resist;
- some lose status but can adapt;
- some gain from transparent access and support;
- some wait because they do not yet know the new system's reliability.

The group cannot be written as a single political mind.
