# V10 Wanming Candidate Test — Cluster A Repair A-P3

> TEST SANDBOX ONLY — NOT CANON  
> Purpose: repair narrative-layer leakage found in A-P3 v1 without changing the organizational mechanism.

## Defect found in v1

A-P3 v1 ended with an explicit conceptual summary roughly equivalent to:

- “this is what real delegation is”
- “easy matters no longer come to him; hard matters finally appear”

The underlying scene already showed the mechanism, so these lines were redundant and partially violated Runtime D1 (现场优先于解释).

This is **not** a candidate-method failure. It is an integration failure: the author explained a mechanism that the scene had already demonstrated.

## A-P3 v2 — revised ending

从“先查”以后，改为：

陈启明把那张纸推回去。

“先查。”

负责协调的人接过纸，没有走。

“另一件呢？”

陈启明这才低头看那张两处争同一批东西的条子。

上面已经列了两个办法。

第一种，先给西边，另一处停半日；第二种，两边各减一半，谁也不至于停，却都会拖慢。

下面还有一行小字。

——西边管事不同意第二种。

“这是谁写的？”

“我。”

“他为何不同意？”

“他说自己昨日已经让过一次。再让，下面的人会觉得他这个管事只是替别人守东西。”

陈启明抬头。

负责协调的人神色有些不自然。

“我说按旧领用数算，他又说旧数不能算这两日的新活。”

外头有人探头，刚要进来，被他回身挡住。

“若还是问日常领料，照新规办。别往这里送。”

门外的人应了一声，又退了出去。

屋里安静下来。

陈启明重新看那张纸。

“你准备怎么定？”

负责协调的人想了一会儿。

“先把两处今天真正要做的活核一遍。若西边不是非用不可，就让。若两边都停不得，我再来报。”

“去吧。”

人走以后，陈启明才发现桌角还压着一张昨日留下的旧条子。

也是问常用耗材。

他拿起来看了看，又放下。

门外已经没有人等他批了。

他伸手把那张旧条子撕掉。

## Why v2 is stronger

- no sentence names or explains the abstract concept “delegation”;
- the coordinator protects the new routing boundary by turning a routine requester away;
- the coordinator has an independent judgment process and local political concern;
- a subordinate's concern about losing face/authority enters the resource decision;
- Chen asks for a decision rather than supplying it;
- the final torn old request is a visible consequence of changed authority, not a narrator conclusion.

## Repair verdict

**PASS**

The candidate mechanism survives removal of explanatory summary.  
Therefore the organizational capability is not dependent on management-report exposition to be intelligible.
