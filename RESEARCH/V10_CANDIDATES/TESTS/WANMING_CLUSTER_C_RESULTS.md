## 3. Cross-capability anti-pattern C-X1

### Target

Do these candidates improve causal bookkeeping while making prose colder, more explanatory and more report-like?

### Result: PASS

Evidence:
- C-P1 communicates the reform cost through a cold stove, waiting sellers and a joke from another vendor; no “治理改善/群众不满” label is needed.
- the ordinary seller inconveniences the protagonist side and forces a change; she is not a gratitude device.
- C-P2 uses rumor mutation and local behavior rather than an author explanation of the information network.
- POV changes are necessary: boatman, workers, merchant, clerk and Chen each add a distinct fragment/action.
- C-P3 does not list consequences. A credit term changes, a trader disappears, a clerk becomes overloaded, and those actions expose the state changes.
- deleting the Test observation sections leaves the fictional scenes fully intelligible.
