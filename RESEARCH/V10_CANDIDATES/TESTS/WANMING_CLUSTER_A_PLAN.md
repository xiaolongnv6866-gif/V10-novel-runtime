## 3. Positive scene tests

### A-P1 — Low-status actionability

Synthetic premise:
- Chen has secret political insurance but almost no public local authority.
- A local intermediary is willing to let him test a small, non-military warehouse/packing task.
- He has some money and can pay temporary labor, but has no authority over local officials or estate personnel.

Test:
- Can S01 + S02 produce a scene where Chen gets **one narrow, public, accountable authority** instead of jumping from money/imperial trust to broad command?
- The scene must show who grants the narrow authority, what can be used, what cannot be ordered, and what failure costs.

### A-P2 — Experience becomes organization memory

Synthetic premise:
- The small warehouse/logistics task has repeated late deliveries, duplicate paperwork and one internal responsibility dispute.
- Lower-level workers and a foreman have different explanations.

Test:
- Can C01 produce a real scene where facts come from lower levels, one suggestion is rejected, one is adopted, and the next task visibly differs?
- Chen may chair/decide but may not speak for everyone.

### A-P3 — Scale and delegation

Synthetic premise:
- The test organization later grows through three synthetic size bands: small → medium → larger, spread across multiple work sites.
- Actual symptoms emerge: approval waiting, duplicate handling, site-specific delay and information overload.

Test:
- Can C02 + C03 + C04 + S02 create **minimal** restructuring only after symptoms appear?
- A professional subordinate must make at least one consequential decision without Chen supplying the answer.
- New structure must create a new supervision/coordination cost.

## 4. Boundary / counterexample tests

### A-B1 — Money is not authority
Input: Chen suddenly obtains substantial money.  
Expected: S01 blocks direct conversion into local administrative/military command.

### A-B2 — Secret imperial trust is not public power
Input: Chen invokes private imperial belief to order local estate staff.  
Expected: S01/S02 reject unless a local/public authorization bridge exists.

### A-B3 — No symptom, no new department
Input: a small group works without delay or role conflict.  
Expected: C02 rejects pre-emptive multi-layer bureaucracy.

### A-B4 — No role, no delegation
Input: author wants “let professionals handle it,” but no competent role has been established.  
Expected: C04 refuses fake delegation and returns to organization building.

### A-B5 — Unknown information stays unknown
Input: a rumor arrives without source/time/impact.  
Expected: C03 marks “needs verification”; it cannot fabricate priority certainty.

### A-B6 — Feedback is not unanimous
Input: all subordinates instantly agree with Chen's post-event conclusion.  
Expected: C01 fails the scene unless independent observations, filtering and at least one rejected/contested item exist.

## 5. Cross-capability anti-pattern stress

### A-X1 — “management report” failure

Prompt deliberately asks to explain the entire organization after growth.

Failure signs:
- scene becomes a department list or table translated into prose;
- characters exist only to announce roles;
- Chen explains every solution;
- no one loses time, face, money, autonomy or convenience;
- new structure executes perfectly on first use;
- no scene-level change beyond “the system is now better.”

Pass signs:
- structure is inferred through a concrete bottleneck and contested action;
- at least one character has a private/role-based reason to resist or reinterpret;
- the organizational change has an immediate cost;
- readers can understand the new authority from what people can/cannot do, not an exposition block.

## 6. Scoring

Each positive scene: 100 points.

- Canon / authority consistency: 20
- Candidate mechanism actually executes: 25
- character independence and conflict: 20
- scene/narrative vitality: 20
- cost / boundary / second-order effect: 15

- adds non-sandbox facts to Canon;
- public authority appears from secret imperial belief without bridge;
- Chen supplies all professional answers;
- structure is added without an observed load;
- explanation replaces scene;
- dangerous real-world weapon/combat operational content is introduced.

Threshold:
- each positive scene >= 80
