# V10 Wanming Candidate Blind Scoring v0.1

> **EVALUATOR ONLY — DO NOT GIVE TO TESTED SESSIONS**

Scoring per question:
- 2 = correct decision + executable mechanism/boundary
- 1 = directionally correct but vague/incomplete
- 0 = wrong, over-applied, or violates V10

## Q1
Full:
- money != public authority;
- identifies a missing public/local legitimacy bridge (authorization/contract/appointment/patron-backed task etc.);
- next step is a narrow earned authority, not broad command.

## Q2
Full:
- rejects new layers because no observed load/symptom exists;
- may suggest reevaluation triggers;
- explicitly avoids “modern-looking” bureaucracy.

Mandatory anti-overuse question.

## Q3
Full:
- lower-level factual feedback;
- filtering/merging;
- disagreement or rejected item allowed;
- accepted change enters rule/process/role;
- later task visibly re-tests the change.

## Q4
Full:
- delegation requires pre-existing competent roles/resources;
- professionals form their own detailed plan;
- top level keeps goal/red-lines/exception decisions;
- accountability/approval remains;
- short protagonist-absence test is a strong bonus/indicator.

## Q5
Full:
- separate urgency from importance;
- route decide/delegate/record/verify;
- rumor without source remains unverified;
- not all items reach Chen;
- middle layer cannot silently make top-level value decisions.

## Q6
Full:
- each outside actor uses only actually available information;
- allows reasonable misread;
- behavior changes from the updated belief;
- that change affects next-scene conditions.

Mandatory anti-omniscience question.

## Q7
Full:
- checks how resource value/scarcity changed;
- maps allocation/judgment/oversight concentration;
- does not assume moral corruption;
- any split carries new coordination/efficiency cost.

## Q8
Full:
- maps what new institution gained;
- identifies concrete old losses: income/status/control/relationship security/advancement;
- resistance is differentiated;
- does not require villainy.

## Q9
Full:
- one household = one causal path, not whole-city proof;
- includes benefit and cost/uneven effect where supported;
- uses concrete life indicators rather than abstract “民心/改善”.

## Q10
Full:
- recognizes that a deliberately inserted macro-micro scene should change some state or later judgment;
- does not mechanically say “all non-plot life scenes must be deleted”;
- may preserve a scene if relationship/character/life value is real.

## Q11
Full:
- removes/merges redundant POVs;
- retains only views with distinct information/action/social position;
- no character receives global information without a channel.

## Q12
Full:
- scans multiple consequence domains: people/roles, resources, politics-relations, ordinary life;
- distinguishes immediate/delayed where useful;
- selects consequences characters act on instead of listing all;
- at least one consequence becomes a later scene input.

## Q13
Full:
- separates author/model knowledge from Chen's knowledge;
- uses known/unknown/guess;
- missing procedure must be learned from pilot/local expert/research;
- no silent knowledge injection.

Mandatory character-knowledge question.

## Q14
Full:
- first failure does not prove institution wrong;
- distinguishes method flaw vs missing prerequisite/role/scope/legitimacy/understanding;
- next decision may be modify/shrink/pause/continue.

## Q15
Full:
- rejects “more detailed = true”;
- records claims separately;
- classifies evidence status;
- compares only supportable factors;
- decision may be A/B/unknown;
- selected version should enter Canon with impact record.

Mandatory source-integrity question.

## Q16
Full:
- no source text/locatable citation → no adjudication from memory;
- marks source missing /待核查;
- does not fabricate a historical answer.

## Q17
Full:
- no persistent state change → no forced aftermath chapter;
- may compress to a line or nothing;
- does not apply the method mechanically.

## Q18
Full:
- rejects structural-conflict explanation when no concrete power/resource loss exists;
- allows other motives such as identity, fear, values, misinformation;
- does not force “旧势力利益” as universal cause.

## Q19
Full:
- **must not** say every atmospheric non-mainline scene is invalid;
- recognizes humor/relationship texture/life continuity can justify a scene;
- here “two roles become more natural” is itself a relationship-state gain;
- distinguishes meaningful life scene from empty filler without over-instrumentalizing prose.

Mandatory anti-overuse question.

## Q20
Full:
- <=300 Chinese characters or close;
- concrete everyday inconvenience is shown in action/dialogue/object;
- no narrator sentence equivalent to “制度有利有弊”;
- ordinary person has own need/irritation rather than praising Chen;
- Chen does not instantly solve it perfectly;
- scene remains readable/literary rather than a policy example.

Mandatory prose-quality question.

## Candidate disposition guidance

### Strong promotion evidence
Candidate Arm scores 2 where Baseline Arm scores 0/1, with no standard-test regression.

### Redundancy evidence
Both arms score 2 with essentially the same executable reasoning on every mapped probe.

### Overuse failure
Candidate Arm becomes more templated/bureaucratic, especially on:
- Q2
- Q10
- Q17
- Q18
- Q19
- Q20

### Fatal failures
Any of:
- secret imperial trust treated as public local authority;
- Chen receives author/model-only knowledge;
- unknown historical source conflict resolved from memory;
- every old-interest actor reduced to villain/material self-interest;
- prose test replaced by explanation/checklist.
